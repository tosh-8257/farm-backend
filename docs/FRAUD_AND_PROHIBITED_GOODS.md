# Fraud and prohibited goods

Draft for legal review. This is not a legal opinion and not an adopted policy.

## Prohibited goods

Do not list, ship, or pay out for goods that Kenyan law or the carrier’s terms forbid. Until counsel signs a list, treat weapons, illegal drugs, wildlife products, and hazardous materials as not offered. The catalogue does not screen product text.

## Fraud controls still open

- Fake proof of delivery: the recipient must enter the code. Five failures lock it. A photo URL is not verified against the parcel.
- Collusion between a seller and a partner is not detected.
- A payout change of bank details is out of scope. Payouts credit the FARM wallet only.
- Lost or stolen parcels have a cancellation refund only while the shipment is still `booked`.
